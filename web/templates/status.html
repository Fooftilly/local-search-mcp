Editable nonsecret settings

History retention and rotation size · Upstream timeouts · Search engine selection · Engine pacing policy · Cache lifetimes · Versioned settings transfer. Nonsecret history, timeout, cache and pacing settings apply to subsequent operations without restart; saving engine selection restarts the verified SearXNG container and can interrupt active searches. An environment override locks the affected form until the override is removed and the bridge is restarted. Domain rules have a separate validated import/export flow on the approvals page.

Port changes must instead be coordinated by the operator across Docker bindings, environment, systemd, tunnel and health monitoring. For safety, no port-edit or start/stop/restart action is provided here.
