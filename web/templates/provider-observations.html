Individually selected local SearXNG searches

Only isolated /search operations actually observed at the MCP's local network boundary are included. Explicitly selected single engines and recovery isolation are counted; cached responses and combined fanout searches are not counted as network operations. A local /search round-trip may include HTTP retries and SearXNG overhead, so it is NOT the upstream provider's execution time or a verified uptime percentage. A results outcome means SearXNG supplied rows bearing the selected engine name. Such rows can still be rejected as unusable by the MCP URL/domain validation; they are not verified end-to-end search successes or proof of provider availability in general.
